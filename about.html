I am a Computer Engineering Student

Hello! I'm Joshua Lagare (known online as Juswa). I am a 4th Year Computer Engineering student from ICCT COLLEGES, working across software development, digital illustration, and embedded systems engineering.

I work with web technologies, Android development, and embedded systems, while exploring digital illustration and UI design along the way.
